The Unessay

Cognitive psychology is not among the most popular subjects for students due to its emphasis on theory and technical nature. In response to this, Goodman (2022) developed an unessay assignment (originated from humanities) for cognitive psychology students. The unessay assignment requires students to create anything but an essay to teach a topic relevant to cognitive psychology that is applicable to real life. This assignment is very enjoyable to mark, I had students create video games, posters, podcasts, video essays, board games, children's books, etc. I observed that students that may not do as well during timed exams or written assignments can have a very successful Unessay, which enriches means of expression in line with the Universal Design for Learning framework.

Due to its unrestricted nature, the unessay can be difficult to grade and students can find the assignment unorthodox. To mitigate, Goodman (2022) recommends sharing a rubric with the students which eases the grading process as well.

I heavily adapted Goodman's rubric and modified it over the semesters - it's certainly a learning curve to administer this assignment, but I think it's worth it. If you wish to download my rubric, click here. (If you are a prospective student, this may prepare you in advance :))
