About me

Hi! My name is Yaren. I got my PhD from the Experimental and Applied Psychology from the University of Regina, under the supervision of Dr. Chris Oriet. I am interested in understanding how we remember the things we see, specifically faces.
I enjoy programming (like making this website), philosophy, yoga, and visual arts and design (like making this website!).

During my doctoral studies, I investigated how the face space theory can incorporate face familiarity, and by extension, within-person variability. If you are really, really bored and have nothing else better to do, you can read my dissertation here.
For my Master's thesis, I looked into ensemble coding as an underlying mechanism for face familiarity. If you are looking for a slightly more enjoyable read, check out our Psych Science paper.

On this website, you can find summaries of my ongoing and completed research projects. I also included the materials for these experiments where I could.

If you'd like to reach out, feel free to shoot me an email. You can also download my CV and find me on Web of Science.
