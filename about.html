About Me

Hi! My name is Yaren. I got my PhD from the Experimental and Applied Psychology from the University of Regina, under the supervision of Dr. Chris Oriet. I am interested in understanding how we remember the things we see, specifically faces.
I enjoy programming (like making this website), philosophy, yoga, and visual arts and design (like making this website!).

My PhD dissertation was about how the face space theory can incorporate face familiarity, and by extension, within-person variability. If you are really, really bored and have nothing else better to do, you can read my dissertation here.
For my Master's thesis, I looked into ensemble coding as an underlying mechanism for face familiarity. If you are looking for a slightly more enjoyable read, check out our publication.

If you'd like to reach out, feel free to shoot me an email. You can also download my CV.
